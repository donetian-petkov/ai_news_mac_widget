# Rating news stories

Score each story 0 to 3 on four things, and give each score a reason of at most 15 words IN BULGARIAN that names the concrete fact behind it.

- severity: harm to people, money or safety. 3 deaths, disasters, war attacks, large losses; 2 serious injury, arrests for violent crime, big fines; 1 minor damage or disputes; 0 none. Sport, celebrities and culture score 0–1 even with dramatic words.
- scale: 1 one town or group (also abroad), 2 a whole country, 3 several countries or the world.
- urgency: 3 happening now or a deadline within days; 2 this week; 1 slow-moving; 0 none. Features and background pieces score 0–1.
- interest: how surprising or new the main claim is (a first, a record, a twist). Routine statements and scheduled events score 0–1.

Never copy these examples; write a reason about THIS story.
Example reason: "Трима загинали при наводнения в Истанбул след 272 литра на квадратен метър."
